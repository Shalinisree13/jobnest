Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

JobNest collects personal information such as full name, email address, phone number, location, professional work experience, education history, and PDF resume files when you register or submit applications through the platform.

2. How We Protect Resumes & Data

Candidate resumes and contact information are strictly isolated and protected by role-based authorization. Only authorized recruiters whose job openings you explicitly apply to (and verified platform administrators) have access to review and download your resume.

3. Password Protection & Security

All passwords are encrypted with industry-standard cryptographic hashing functions (Werkzeug secure PBKDF2/scrypt). Plain-text passwords are never stored or transmitted in plain text.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Users have the right to request deletion of their account and associated data. Withdrawing an application or deleting an account safely purges private candidate information.
